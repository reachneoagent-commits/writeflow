Writeflow isn't signed with an Apple Developer ID yet, so macOS blocks the first launch. Either:

1) Drag Writeflow to Applications and double-click it once (dismiss the warning), then open
   System Settings -> Privacy & Security, scroll down and click "Open Anyway", and confirm with your password.
2) Or run this once in Terminal (it removes the download flag):
   xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Writeflow.app

After that, double-clicking Writeflow opens it in your browser. It has no window and no Dock icon;
quit from the "..." menu at the top of the page.
